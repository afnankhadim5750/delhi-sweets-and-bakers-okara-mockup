A landmark on Jinnah Road

Okara stops here
for something sweet.

Ask anyone in Okara where to buy mithai and the answer comes back the same: Delhi Sweets & Bakers, on Mohammad Ali Jinnah Road. It is the shop people point visitors to, the stop on the way home, and the counter that has fed the city's celebrations for as long as most customers can remember.

What keeps the queue moving is simple. The barfi and qalaqand are the house specialities — the two sweets reviewers praise above everything else. The chicken samosa has its own following, crisp from the fryer and gone by the tray. And behind the same counter sits a bakery turning out cakes and everyday bakes.

  • Barfi & qalaqand — the house specialities
  • Chicken samosa — a local favourite
  • Gulqand & traditional mithai — for every occasion
  • Cakes & bakery items — baked fresh through the day

Weddings, Eid & family gatherings start at this counter.
